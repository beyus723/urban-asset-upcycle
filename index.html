The Process

Simple. Transparent. Beneficial to all.

Meanwhile use is not a loophole — it is a legitimate, council-supported mechanism to bring empty buildings back to life while delivering real financial benefit to property owners.

1

We Find Your Building

We identify vacant commercial properties and approach owners with a detailed rates saving calculation specific to their property — real numbers, no guesswork.

2

We Agree a Meanwhile Licence

A simple, flexible licence agreement — typically 6–24 months with clear notice periods on both sides. No complex legal fees for you. We use solicitor-drafted standard documents.

3

We Apply for Rates Relief

As a registered charity in occupation, we apply for 80% mandatory charitable relief — and the discretionary 20% top-up. Your liability reduces from day one of our occupation.

4

We Activate the Space

Within 30 days we open the building for community use — workshops, training, pop-ups, studios. The property is maintained, secured, and contributing to the local economy.

5

You Get Your Building Back

When you're ready — a tenant arrives, planning permission granted, sale agreed — we vacate professionally on agreed notice. Clean exit, impact report provided, relationship maintained.

6

Everyone Wins

You saved thousands in rates. The community gained a resource. We advanced our charitable mission. The high street looks better. That is the meanwhile use model at its best.

For Property Owners

What you gain from meanwhile use

This is a genuine financial arrangement — not philanthropy. You receive measurable benefits in exchange for temporary access.

💷
Dramatic Rates Reduction

80% mandatory relief the moment we take occupation. We apply on your behalf and keep you informed throughout the process.

🔒
Property Security

An occupied, active building is far less likely to be vandalised, broken into, or attract squatters. We heat and maintain the interior.

📋
Flexible Exit

Our licences include clear notice clauses — typically 2–3 months. If a commercial tenant arrives or planning consent is granted, we vacate professionally.

🌱
Community Goodwill

Your building becomes a visible community asset. Local press, social media, and council relationships all benefit from association with positive regeneration.

📊
Full Transparency

We provide regular updates, a copy of the rates relief confirmation, and an impact report on exit. You always know what's happening in your building.

⚖️
Legally Sound

All agreements are solicitor-drafted. Occupation is genuine and active — we meet all Charity Commission and council requirements for rates relief.

What We Activate

The buildings we work with

We focus on commercial properties that have been vacant for 3+ months and have a rateable value where the rates saving makes meanwhile use compelling.

🏪
Former Retail Units

High street and shopping parade units are ideal. Street-level access means immediate community visibility. We transform them into markets, studios, training hubs, and pop-up spaces.

🏢
Vacant Office Floors

Upper floors above retail or standalone office buildings work well for desk space, meeting rooms, and skills training. Often overlooked — and often carrying significant rates liability.

🍺
Closed Pubs & Venues

Former pubs and community venues carry strong community attachment. Meanwhile use keeps them alive, maintains their asset value, and creates a positive narrative for redevelopment.

🏦
Former Banks & Post Offices

High-profile buildings with strong bones and prominent locations. Their closure often leaves a visible gap on the high street — meanwhile use fills it meaningfully.

🏭
Industrial Units

Units awaiting planning or redevelopment can serve as maker spaces, workshop hubs, or storage for community organisations — while saving the landlord ongoing rates liability.

🏗️
Pre-development Sites

Buildings in the gap between planning approval and construction start are perfect for meanwhile use. We bridge the gap, maintain the property, and hand back on your timetable.
